# Cover a gap

1. The shift: `week_schedule` or `who_is_on` for the day; confirm the position, start and end.
2. `coverage_candidates` — eligible (position, main restaurant, available, no hard rule), free, fewest hours first,
   with any soft warnings.
3. `coverage_offer` to the best two or three together, with a one-line note ("Tonight 5–11 Server — Priya is sick").
4. Tell the manager or shift lead who was asked. The first to accept gets the shift; the others are told it is gone.
5. Nobody within the hour and the shift is soon: say so plainly and list the next candidates — do not keep asking.

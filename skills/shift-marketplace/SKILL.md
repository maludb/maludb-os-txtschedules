# The shift marketplace

- **Offer** — the person working a published shift puts it up. It stays theirs until someone takes it (and, if the
  restaurant requires, a manager approves). Nobody takes it → it expires and stays theirs.
- **Pickup** — take an offered shift or an open one. Only staff whose **main restaurant** it is can take it, only if
  they work the position and no hard rule stops them.
- **Swap** — my shift for a named colleague's shift at the same restaurant; they accept or decline; then, if required,
  a manager decides. Both people are checked, each for the other's shift.
- **Give** — my shift to one named colleague, who accepts or declines.
- **Coverage** — a manager or shift lead offers a gap to one or several eligible people; the first to accept gets it,
  no further approval.

## The restaurant's trade settings
Each kind can be turned off. Each can need a manager **always**, **never**, or **only when a warning fires** (the
default). No exchange closer to the start than the **cutoff** (default two hours). **First claim wins** (default) or
the **manager chooses** among claimants. Shift leads may approve same-day trades when the restaurant lets them.

## When two people try at once
Exactly one wins; the other is told the shift is gone. When refused, say the reason as the tool gives it.
